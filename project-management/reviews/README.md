# Review Records

The project has adopted a standing nine-lens Panel of Experts as an
exceptional mechanism for major, cross-cutting decisions. Its controlling
operating standard is the
[`Panel Of Experts Charter`](panel-of-experts-charter.md).

The Panel is advisory. It does not approve work by vote or accept risk.
Readiness, deferral, rejection, and accepted risk remain explicit operator
decisions.

Routine implementation, bug fixes, tests, documentation, and mechanical
resolution of reviewed findings use normal verification and focused peer
review. A formal Panel is explicitly convened with a proportional odd subset
of three, five, or nine relevant lenses. Nine seats are reserved for
foundational or product-wide architecture.

Records live here:

- `packets/` contains immutable, versioned material supplied identically to
  every reviewer;
- `findings/` contains raw, independently attributable findings and explicit
  no-finding reports; and
- `decisions/` contains evidence-chair dispositions and separate operator
  decisions.

The project charter specializes FieldManual's under-review risk-triggered
expert-board proposal. The FieldManual proposal remains informative and does
not itself govern this project.

The first review,
[`EXPL-REV-001`](packets/EXPL-REV-001-foundation-design-v1.md), was the
comprehensive nine-seat initial foundation review. Its detailed artifacts are
retained as historical evidence; their administrative shape is not the
default for future reviews.
